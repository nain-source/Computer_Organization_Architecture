REGISTER MAPPING
t0 = x5     t1 = x6     t2 = x7
t3 = x28    t4 = x29    t5 = x30

R-TYPE FORMAT
funct7 | rs2 | rs1 | funct3 | rd | opcode

R-type opcode = 0110011

Instruction       funct7    rs2   rs1   funct3   rd   opcode
add t2,t0,t1       0000000    6     5     000     7    0110011
sub t3,t0,t1       0100000    6     5     000    28    0110011
mul t4,t0,t1       0000001    6     5     000    29    0110011
mul t5,t0,t0       0000001    5     5     000    30    0110011

I-TYPE INSTRUCTIONS
The program also uses ADDI instructions to initialize A and B.

I-type format:
immediate | rs1 | funct3 | rd | opcode

ADDI opcode = 0010011

Instruction        Immediate   rs1   funct3   rd   opcode
addi t0,x0,5        5           0     000      5    0010011
addi t1,x0,4        4           0     000      6    0010011
